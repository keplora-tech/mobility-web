Four ideas.
One fundamental shift.

Every network that changed the world worked this way.

01
Join once.

A single connection to Mobility Web makes any mobility service immediately reachable by every other service on the network. It connects to a shared layer, not individual partners.

02
Reach everything.

New services become available across the ecosystem the moment they join.

03
Grow beyond mobility.

As more services join, the city's mobility layer becomes part of a broader network of commerce, logistics, energy and more.

04
Nothing in the middle.

Mobility Web defines how services talk. Like TCP/IP, it sits beneath every connection it enables.

What it enables

Not just what
you build today.

The value of open infrastructure is what others build on it — without asking permission.

What nobody predicted

Open layers create conditions for solutions nobody designed — including AI-native interfaces that discover, transact, and coordinate across a city in real time.

Every new mode, already connected

Autonomous vehicles, urban aviation, and energy-as-transport join the same layer without rebuilding the connections. The infrastructure grows with the city, not behind it.

Compounding value

Every new service that joins increases the value for every other service already connected. The open standard gets more powerful the more it is used.

On the NFH Fabric

Vouch directs.
Pincer guarantees.

Mobility Web is part of a complete open infrastructure stack from NFH. Vouch adds purpose-bound value — directing funds and benefits to the right recipients and outcomes. Pincer adds cryptographic trust — guaranteeing every transaction settles the moment proof conditions are met.

Purpose-bound value for any programme. Money, credit, or equity issued with embedded rules — directed to the right recipient, usable in the right context, tracked to the right outcome.

Networks for Humanity (NFH) is a global non-profit initiative with a bold vision to provide open, interoperable and decentralized digital infrastructure that enables broader access and participation in the digital economy. The NFH Fabric powers Mobility Web, providing the open standard that any mobility service can connect to.

WRI is a global research organisation working to improve people's lives, protect nature and stabilise the climate. They bring city relationships, policy expertise, and on-the-ground research that makes Mobility Web work in practice.
